# Security and Compliance

> Reviewed by the compliance team. Do not edit without their approval.

BacancyCare EHR is built to align with HIPAA and HITECH.

## Deployment and data ownership
The platform runs on the customer's own servers or a private cloud account in the customer's name. Bacancy does not hold patient records.

## Safeguards
| Area | Details |
|---|---|
| Encryption | AES-256 at rest and in transit |
| Video visits | End-to-end encrypted (SRTP/DTLS). No PHI is stored on the video provider's systems. |
| Audit logs | Every view and change of PHI is logged, with 7-year retention |
| Access | Role-based access, minimum-necessary PHI |
| Payments | PCI-DSS gateways |

## Standards and frameworks
HIPAA · HITECH · 21st Century Cures Act information-blocking rules · CMS-0057-F (FHIR Da Vinci PAS) · TEFCA QHIN ready · NIST AI RMF · NIST SP 800-66 · California AB 3030 · GDPR / UK GDPR

## Agreements
- **BAA:** a signed Business Associate Agreement is provided with every license. [Compliance: confirm "every license" vs "on request"]
- **DPA:** available for UK and Australia.

## AI governance
- AI photo review is labelled in the app as not a diagnosis.
- Visit transcripts are saved only with patient consent.
- Urgent and uncertain cases go to staff.

## Company certification
Bacancy Technology, the delivery organization, is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

## Words we do not use
We do not say "HIPAA certified", because no official HIPAA certification exists.
