## What patients can do
Book visits, check insurance, see costs, join video visits, pay bills and track pharmacy orders.

## Book a visit
1. Choose how to start: consult now, urgent visit, symptom check, or find a doctor.
2. If you use symptom check, describe your symptoms. You'll be matched to a suitable doctor.
3. Pick the visit type: in person, video or phone.
4. Review the insurance check and the cost estimate.
5. Pay online, in cash at the clinic, or by insurance.

You can book as a guest. Your account is created during booking.

## After the visit
- Download your prescription PDF.
- Track your pharmacy order from New to Completed, then pick it up or have it delivered.
- See your payment history.
