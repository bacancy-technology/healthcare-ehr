# Physician Guide

## What you do in Bacancy EHR
Review the chart, consult, prescribe and order labs.

## Start a visit
1. Open **Schedule** and click the patient's appointment.
2. Review the chart: history, last visit, results and RPM readings.
3. For a video visit, click **Start teleconsultation**.

## Write a prescription
1. In the chart, open **Prescriptions → New**.
2. Choose the medicine, dose and duration.
3. Choose the patient's pharmacy.
4. Click **Send**.

## Order a lab test
1. In the chart, open **Orders → New lab order**.
2. Choose the tests and the lab.
3. Click **Send**. The result appears in the chart when the lab returns it.
