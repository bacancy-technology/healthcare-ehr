# BacancyCare EHR Pricing

**Last updated: 6 Nov 2026.** The official, current prices are on [bacancycare.com/ehr](https://bacancycare.com/ehr?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=ehr&utm_content=pricing-top). If they differ, the website is correct.

## Two ways to pay

| Option | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Own it** (one-time license) | **$10,000 – $40,000** | Practices that want to own the software and keep costs flat over the long term |
| **Subscribe** (monthly) | **$150 – $400 per doctor per month** | Practices that prefer a lower starting cost |

The final price depends on the number of doctors, locations, portals and integrations.

## Example: own vs subscribe
A 3-provider practice over 5 years:

| | Total over 5 years |
|---|---|
| Subscribe | $27,000 |
| Own | $22,500 |
